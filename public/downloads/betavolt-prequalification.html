About BetaVolt

Company Overview & Philosophy

Corporate Overview

BetaVolt is a specialized engineering and contracting company incorporated under the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We provide end-to-end electrical, energy, and smart infrastructure solutions for industrial, commercial, and institutional clients across the Kingdom.

Operating at the intersection of precision engineering and advanced technology, BetaVolt designs, builds, and commissions systems that power, protect, and connect modern facilities. Our integrated delivery model covers every project phase — from feasibility and design through procurement, installation, testing, commissioning, and long-term maintenance.

Every BetaVolt project is executed under an uncompromising commitment to quality, safety, and performance — ensuring that our clients receive infrastructure that meets international standards and lasts for decades.

What We Deliver

Departments & Core Services

01 ——
Power & Electrical Infrastructure
Design, supply and installation of complete MV/LV electrical systems for industrial plants, commercial towers and institutional campuses.
  • MV/LV switchgear, distribution boards & busbar trunking
  • Power factor correction & harmonic filtration systems
  • Emergency power — generators, UPS & ATS systems
  • Earthing, bonding & lightning protection systems
02 ——
Solar Energy Systems (EPC)
End-to-end photovoltaic solutions from feasibility and yield analysis through full EPC delivery and long-term O&M management.
  • On-grid, off-grid & hybrid PV systems
  • Rooftop, ground-mount & carport installations
  • String & central inverter configurations
  • Solar SCADA monitoring & performance analytics
03 ——
Low Current & Smart Systems
Comprehensive ICT and security infrastructure for modern facilities requiring connectivity, visibility and integrated control.
  • Structured cabling — Cat6A, OM3/OM4 fiber optic
  • IP CCTV & video analytics systems
  • Access control & intrusion detection
  • Addressable fire alarm & detection systems
04 ——
Building Management Systems (BMS)
Centralized intelligence integrating HVAC, lighting, power and security into a unified control layer for complex facilities.
  • BACnet / Modbus / KNX protocol integration
  • HVAC automation & energy optimization
  • Lighting control & occupancy-based scheduling
  • Remote monitoring dashboards & automated alerts
05 ——
Data Centers & Server Rooms
Purpose-built mission-critical infrastructure engineered for maximum uptime, efficiency and future scalability.
  • Precision cooling — CRAC/CRAH & in-row cooling
  • Redundant power distribution (Tier II–III design)
  • Structured cabling & intelligent patch management
  • Environmental monitoring & physical security
06 ——
Industrial Automation — PLC & SCADA
Advanced process control for industrial operations — from single-machine control to plant-wide supervision and digitalization.
  • PLC programming: Siemens, Allen-Bradley, Schneider
  • SCADA design & HMI development
  • Motor control centers (MCC) & VFD integration
  • Instrumentation & field device integration
07 ——
Network Infrastructure
Resilient high-performance network backbone for enterprise, industrial and campus environments.
  • Core / distribution / access layer architecture
  • Managed switching, routing & wireless (WiFi 6/6E)
  • Network security: firewall, segmentation & VPN
  • Passive infrastructure — racks, patch panels & UPS
08 ——
Operations & Maintenance (O&M)
Protecting infrastructure investment through proactive maintenance programs and rapid-response technical support.
  • Planned preventive maintenance (PPM) schedules
  • 24/7 emergency breakdown response capability
  • System upgrades & capacity expansion programs
  • Energy audits & efficiency optimization studies
